Scope: `03_rsosh_selected.yaml`, `04_ctf_cup_russia.yaml`,
`07_rsosh_informatics_2026.yaml`, `11_law_olympiads.yaml`,
`12_economics_olympiads.yaml`, and `13_rosfin_olympiad.yaml`.

Every existing event was rechecked against the organizer's own site. `confirmed
unchanged` means the existing published 2026/27 information still matches; `TBD
unchanged` means that no current-season timetable was published and no prior-season
date was carried forward; `inaccessible` means the official page could not be read
by the review client, so the existing conservative record was retained. No dates,
RSOSH level, or new stage was inferred. Event IDs, progression links, and `terminal`
markers were unchanged.

  tour for Informatics and Industrial Programming, and invites registered
  participants to tour two. The stored date-only milestone alternatives remain
  `tentative`; 13–22 November and 5–15 February keep exclusive ends on 23 November
  and 16 February.
- [The AI Olympiad 2026 page](https://ai.edu.gov.ru/) confirms registration and
  training through 20 September, qualifier 21 September–6 October, main stage
  14–18 October, and final 16–21 November. Its `RSOSH` placement remains preliminary,
  not a claim of an approved 2026/27 list.
- [CTF Cup Russia](https://ctfcup.ru/) confirms its qualifying tour on
  31 October–1 November 2026; its exclusive end is therefore 2 November.
  `source_kind: OTHER` is intentional.
- [Rosfin stages](https://rosfinolymp.ru/stages) confirms the school-only dates:
  round one 14 March, round two 19 April 10:00–14:00 MSK, motivation letters
  1 June–2 September, selection 2–3 September, and final 27 September–2 October.
  [The participant page](https://rosfinolymp.ru/for-participants) confirms the
  qualifying round is for grades 8–10. Existing exclusive window ends are correct.
